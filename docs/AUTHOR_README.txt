HD2 Dress {version}: engine cloth for Helldivers 2 armour (preview for testers)

Real cloth physics (the game's own cape system) on your armour: skirts, capes, coat tails, sleeves, hair, ribbons.
Players need no extra mod or loader: they install your patch files like any armour mod. Cloth catches the
Super Destroyer breeze like the stock cape (Wind slider, 0 = off).

1. Install HD2-Dress-Blender-{version}.zip in Blender 4.0-5.2 (Edit > Preferences > Add-ons > Install from Disk),
   next to HD2SDK Community Edition. Windows: needs the Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64) for texture
   compression (without it the build still works, textures are just larger).

2. Open GUIDE.html in your browser (step by step, pictures included in the file).

Recent: Fit to my model and Show colliders (3 Physics > Body fit) fit the invisible body colliders to your model and
draw them over it; Add back side (4 Look) for cloth whose inside can be seen; Check finds meshes on a moved origin
and separate double layers, and Fix all repairs the origin.

reference/ holds the detailed docs (every setting, the tested values and why).

Preview: please report what breaks, what confuses, and screenshots of anything odd.
